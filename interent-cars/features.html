02 — Features

Five ways we changed the car.

Interent Cars doesn't tweak the dealership model — it replaces it. Here are the five capabilities that make that possible, and what each one closes in the traditional market.

01

Transparent real-time market pricing

Advertised prices and transaction prices are rarely the same. Market adjustments, documentation fees, and add-on packages obscure the real cost until it is too late for the buyer to walk away easily.

What we deploy
  • Live pricing engine pulling real transaction data from 200,000+ recent sales nationwide.
  • All-in price display — tax, registration, documentation, and delivery included up front.
  • "Fair Deal" badge when a listing falls within 2% of the 30-day market average.
  • Price history chart so buyers can see how a vehicle has been priced over time.
02

Same-session financing with no rate markup

Dealer-arranged financing silently adds 1–3% to the loan rate — a cost invisible at signing but worth thousands over the loan term. Buyers who pre-approve elsewhere face friction and delay.

What we deploy
  • Soft-pull pre-qualification in under 2 minutes — zero credit score impact.
  • Direct lender partnerships across 40+ banks and credit unions — no dealer middleman.
  • Rate comparison panel: see every offer side by side before committing.
  • Digital closing documents: sign your loan agreement without visiting a branch.
03

AI-powered vehicle matching

Most car search tools are little more than filter bars — they surface what the dealer has, not what the buyer needs. Without contextual intelligence, buyers default to brand loyalty and guess at value.

What we deploy
  • 40-parameter recommendation model: commute distance, family size, fuel cost, insurance risk, resale curve.
  • Total cost of ownership calculator baked into every listing — not just the sticker.
  • "Switch savings" analysis: what you'd save moving from your current car to this one.
  • Alerts when a better-matched vehicle hits the market at a price that beats your current shortlist.
04

Verified inventory & door-to-door delivery

Online listings are routinely outdated, inaccurate, or bait-and-switch. Buyers drive hours only to discover the car doesn't match the photos, has undisclosed damage, or has already been sold.

What we deploy
  • Every listing verified: 150-point inspection report, full service history, clean title guarantee.
  • 360° video walkthrough with time-stamped defect markers — no hidden surprises.
  • Nationwide delivery to your door within 72 hours — fully insured in transit.
  • 7-day / 500-mile return window: if the car isn't right, we take it back. No questions.
05

Connected ownership & resale intelligence

Car ownership ends at the handshake. Maintenance reminders come from dealers with a conflict of interest, recall alerts arrive late, and resale happens blind — with no leverage and no data.

What we deploy
  • Owner dashboard: live resale value tracking, maintenance timeline, and OBD-linked health score.
  • Recall and safety alert push notifications — routed directly from NHTSA, no dealer delay.
  • Trade-in offer locked in at purchase — guaranteed for 12 months, updated monthly thereafter.
  • One-tap re-list: when you're ready to sell, your full verified history is already on the platform.
