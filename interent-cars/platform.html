03 — Platform

The car as a connected platform.

Not a website that lists cars — a vertically integrated marketplace that prices, finances, delivers, and manages car ownership from first search to final sale.

Who We Serve

Three stakeholders. One connected system.

The Buyer

Transparent, frictionless, front-door delivery

Search, finance, and take delivery of a verified vehicle without ever visiting a dealership — with full price transparency and a 7-day return window.

The Seller / Dealer

Demand-matched inventory and qualified leads

List inventory against real demand signals, receive pre-approved buyer leads, and reduce days-on-lot through dynamic pricing recommendations.

The Lender

AI-underwritten, low-default loan origination

Access a curated pipeline of pre-screened borrowers with verified income, employment, and vehicle-to-value ratios — originate at lower cost with better data.

Layer 01

Live market data engine

Our pricing database ingests over 200,000 real transaction records monthly from dealer management systems, auction results, and verified private sales. Every listing on Interent Cars is priced against actual — not asking — market data, updated every 24 hours.

  • +200K+ monthly transaction data points
  • +VIN-level history integration (Carfax, AutoCheck)
  • +Regional price elasticity modelling

Layer 02

AI underwriting & lender marketplace

Our underwriting model evaluates 120+ signals — traditional credit, open banking income verification, employment continuity, and vehicle-specific default risk — to generate a decisioned offer in under 90 seconds. Buyers see competing rates from every willing lender before choosing.

  • +Sub-90-second pre-approval
  • +40+ direct lender integrations
  • +Zero rate markup — lender pays us, not the buyer
Layer 03

Verified inventory & logistics network

Every vehicle on the platform has passed a 150-point physical inspection, has a verified title, and carries a clean accident disclosure. Our fulfilment network of certified carrier partners delivers nationwide in 72 hours — tracked end-to-end.

  • +150-point pre-listing inspection
  • +72-hour nationwide delivery SLA
  • +7-day / 500-mile return guarantee
