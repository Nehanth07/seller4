Market Tailwinds

Four structural forces that make this moment irreversible.

  • 01

    Millennial & Gen-Z buyers expect end-to-end digital

    Buyers under 40 — now the majority — grew up purchasing everything online and are deeply uncomfortable with coercive, time-consuming in-person negotiation.

  • 02

    Post-pandemic shift to remote purchasing is permanent

    COVID-19 forced dealers to offer home delivery and remote signing. Buyers tried it, preferred it, and now expect it as standard.

  • 03

    Open banking unlocks real-time income verification

    API-level access to bank data allows instant, accurate underwriting that was impossible 5 years ago — dramatically reducing default risk for lenders and friction for buyers.

  • 04

    EV transition resets buyer loyalties

    As buyers switch to EVs, brand loyalty and established dealer relationships restart from zero — creating a rare window to capture first-time digital buyers before habits reform.

The Model

Three revenue lines. One network effect.

Transaction Revenue

Flat success fee per completed sale

We earn a fixed fee per transaction — aligning our incentive with the buyer getting the right car at the right price, not with inflating the deal.

Financing Revenue

Lender referral fee — zero buyer markup

Lenders pay us for qualified, verified loan applicants. Buyers never pay a rate premium — our model is funded by the lenders who win lower-risk originations.

Subscription Revenue

Owner dashboard & resale intelligence

Post-purchase connected ownership features — live resale tracking, maintenance intelligence, and instant re-list — generate recurring subscription revenue from every owner.
