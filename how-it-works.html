Interrentcars — The Process

How it works.

From browse to boulevard in four simple steps. No paperwork queues, no hidden fees.

01

Choose your car

Browse the full fleet and pick the vehicle that fits your journey — from executive sedans to supercars.

02

Pick dates & location

Select your pickup and return, or request door-to-door delivery to your home, hotel or airport.

03

Verify & book

Upload your driver's license and ID once. Secure your reservation in under two minutes.

04

Drive away

Your fully detailed, fuelled and insured car arrives on time. All that's left is to enjoy the drive.

What you'll need

01

Driver's License

A valid driver's license is required to rent and legally operate any InterRent vehicle.

02

ID Card

A government-issued ID card is used to confirm your identity at the time of pickup.

03

Passport

International renters can present a valid passport as primary proof of identity.
